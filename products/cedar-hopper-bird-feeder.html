About this one

This is the feeder we reach for first, and it is the one we stock the most of. The body is western red cedar left unfinished, so it greys slowly rather than peeling. Seed goes in through the hinged roof and feeds down to a 1-inch lip on both long sides, which gives four or five birds room to stand without crowding each other off.

The acrylic side panels let you see the seed level from a window, so you are not walking out in February to find out it is empty. The tray floor is drilled with six drainage holes, and the whole feeder lifts off its hanger for cleaning. It ships with a 14-inch galvanised hanging chain; it also sits on a standard 1-inch pole socket if you would rather post-mount it.

Fill it with black oil sunflower, a mixed seed, or safflower. We do not make any claim about which birds will turn up at your particular yard, because that depends on your trees, your neighbours' feeders and the time of year far more than it depends on the feeder.

Care
  • Empty completely and scrub with a stiff brush and warm soapy water every two to three weeks.
  • Rinse well and let it dry fully in the sun before refilling.
  • Leave the cedar bare, or wipe it once a season with a clear exterior penetrating oil.
  • Do not put it in a dishwasher.
