About this one

Six packets, each one a single named species rather than a mystery mix, because a mix gives you no way to know what came up and no way to reorder the one you liked. The collection is cosmos, California poppy, cornflower, black-eyed Susan, purple coneflower and lance-leaf coreopsis.

The first three are annuals that flower the same season you sow them. The last three are perennials — they will put on leaf the first year and flower properly from the second, which is how perennials work and worth knowing before you judge them in August.

Each packet carries the botanical name, the seed count, sowing depth, spacing, the weeks to germination, and the packing season. The packets are paper, not foil, so keep them somewhere cool and dry and sow within the season printed on them.

We do not print a germination-rate guarantee on these, because germination depends on your soil temperature, your watering and your weather far more than on the seed.
