About this one

This box is built to the dimensions the bluebird societies publish: a 1.5-inch entry hole, a 4 by 4-inch floor, and the hole set 6 inches above that floor. There is deliberately no perch below the hole, because a perch mainly helps the birds you were not hoping for.

The right-hand side panel pivots on two screws so you can open it in autumn and clear the old nest out. The roof overhangs the front by 2 inches to keep rain off the hole, the floor is recessed and drilled for drainage, and the inside face below the hole is scored with saw kerfs so fledglings can climb out.

Mount it 4 to 6 feet up on a post or a smooth pole, facing away from prevailing weather, with open ground in front of it. Whether bluebirds use it is up to the bluebirds.
