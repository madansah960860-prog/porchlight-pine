About this one

Seed starting fails for one of two reasons: the compost dries out, or it never dries out. A base tray plus a vented dome is how you control both, and that is the whole kit.

You get two 18-cell inserts — 36 cells in total, each 1.5 inches square and 2.25 inches deep, which is enough root room for tomatoes, peppers, brassicas and most flowers up to transplant. Each cell has a drainage slot in the base. The inserts sit in one solid tray with no holes, so you water from underneath and let the compost wick it up, which is far gentler on new roots than watering over the top.

The dome is 2.5 inches tall with two sliding vents. Keep them shut until the seed leaves are up, then open them gradually. The whole thing is 21 by 11 inches, which fits a standard windowsill or a single shop light.

The inserts are reusable for several seasons if you wash them between sowings. No seed or compost is included.

Care
  • Wash the inserts and tray in warm soapy water between sowings and rinse well.
  • Water into the base tray and let the compost wick it up rather than watering over the top.
  • Open the dome vents once the seed leaves are up; take the dome off entirely after that.
  • Store the dome flat so it does not warp. Not dishwasher safe.
