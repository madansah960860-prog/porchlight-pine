About this one

Two things that get lost and therefore get bought twice: gloves and pruners. Buying them together is not a gimmick, it is just how it usually goes.

The gloves have a goatskin leather palm with a breathable knit back and a short elasticated cuff. Goatskin is the useful compromise — supple enough that you can still feel a stem, tough enough that a rose does not go straight through it. They come in one size that fits most adult hands; the measurement across the knuckles is 3.9 inches, so you can check against your own.

The shears are a true bypass pattern, meaning the blades cross like scissors rather than crushing against an anvil, which is what you want on a living stem. Maximum cut is 0.75 inch of green wood. There is a sap groove along the blade, a wire notch at the base, a coil spring, and a locking catch that works with a thumb.

Care
  • Wipe the shear blades after each use and clean sap off with a little mineral spirits.
  • Put one drop of light oil on the pivot bolt every few uses.
  • Touch the bevel up with a fine diamond file — sharpen the outer face only.
  • Let the gloves air dry away from direct heat. Do not machine wash or tumble dry the leather palm.
