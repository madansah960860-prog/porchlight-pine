About this one

Suet cages are one of those things where the cheap ones rust through in a season and the good ones last a decade, and the only difference is the coating. These are vinyl-coated steel, so they do not leave rust streaks down the side of a shed and they do not get sharp at the welds.

Each cage takes one standard 11-ounce suet cake — the rectangular kind sold in most feed stores — and the top panel hinges all the way back so you can drop a cake in without breaking it in half. The latch is a simple hook that a raccoon can work out, so if that is your situation, thread the hook with a short zip tie.

You get two in the pack because it is worth hanging one near a window and one further out.

Care
  • Empty and scrub with a stiff brush and warm soapy water every three to four weeks.
  • Dry fully before refilling so the coating does not trap moisture at the welds.
  • In hot weather use a no-melt suet dough rather than a plain rendered cake.
