About this one

This is the set for somebody setting up a shed from nothing, or replacing five mismatched tools with five that match. All five heads are carbon steel on riveted tangs, and all five handles are the same turned hardwood, so they hang in a row and feel the same in your hand.

The transplanter is the one people are surprised by: it is a narrow 2-inch blade with depth marks stamped every inch up the face, which turns "plant it about three inches down" into something you can actually check. The cultivator has three curved tines for breaking a crust on a bed, the weeder is a forked prong for taprooted weeds, and the dibber is a plain tapered point for bulbs and leeks.

Nothing here is a novelty. Five tools you will use, in one box.
