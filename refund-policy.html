Returns & refunds

30 days from delivery, change of mind included. Who pays the postage, how refunds work, and what we cannot take back.

Effective date: September 27, 2026. This policy applies to orders placed through porchlightandpine.com and to telephone orders taken on (828) 555-0142.

The short version

You have 30 days from the day your order is delivered to send anything back, provided it is unused and in its original packaging. If the fault is ours we pay the postage both ways and refund you in full. If you simply changed your mind, you pay the return postage and we refund the price of the goods. We never charge a restocking fee, and this policy covers ordinary change-of-mind returns and not only faulty goods.

Everything below is the detail. If any of it is unclear, call (828) 555-0142 and ask.

The return window

The 30 days run from the delivery date shown by the carrier, not from the day you ordered and not from the day you opened the box. Your return request must reach us inside those 30 days; the parcel itself then has a further 14 days to reach us.

If you are giving something as a gift and the 30 days will run out before the occasion, tell us when you order and we will note a later window on the account. We would rather do that than argue about it in February.

Condition we need it back in

- Unused. Not installed outdoors, not filled with seed, nectar or water, not planted into, not used to dig. - Complete. All parts, all hardware, the instruction sheet, and the original box or bag it came in. - Undamaged by the customer. Normal handling while you decided is fine; a scratched blade or a chipped rim is not.

We do not require the outer shipping carton to be pristine - we expect you to have opened it. We do need the product's own packaging, because that is what protects it on the way back.

If something arrives back to us used or incomplete, we will photograph it, email you, and offer you either a partial refund reflecting the condition or the item returned to you at our expense. We will not simply keep it and refuse the refund.

Items we cannot take back

- Wildflower Seed Collection, Six Packets - once any packet has been opened. Unopened collections with all six packets sealed are fully returnable. Seed is a living product and we cannot resell it once it has been out of its packet. - Anything installed outdoors. A feeder that has been hung and filled, a nest box that has been screwed to a tree, a bird bath that has been sited and watered, or a planter that has been planted cannot come back. Assemble and inspect indoors first if you are unsure about it. - Items sold as final sale. We rarely do this, and when we do the product page says so in plain words above the Add to Cart button. Nothing on the site is final sale at the time of writing.

Nothing in this section applies to a faulty or damaged item. A faulty item is always returnable, including seed packets that arrive split and feeders that arrive cracked.

How to start a return

1. Email hello@porchlightandpine.com with the subject line "Return" within 30 days of delivery. Or telephone (828) 555-0142 during Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm Eastern Time and we will do it with you on the call. 2. Tell us four things: your order number (it looks like PP-10432 and is in your confirmation email), which item you are returning, how many, and whether it is faulty or a change of mind. If it is faulty, attach a photograph. 3. We reply within one business day with a Return Authorisation number, the return address, and - if the fault is ours - a prepaid shipping label as a PDF. 4. Write the RA number on the outside of the parcel, pack the item in its own original packaging inside an outer box, and send it. Use a service with tracking; we cannot refund a return we never receive. 5. Email us the tracking number. We will tell you when it arrives.

Please do not send anything back without a Return Authorisation number. It is not bureaucracy for its own sake - unlabelled parcels arrive at a building that also receives stock, and they get lost.

Who pays return postage

We pay - prepaid label, full refund including your original shipping charge :: The item arrived damaged. The item is faulty or fails in normal use inside the window. We sent the wrong thing. The item is materially different from its description on this site. You pay - we refund the price of the goods, not the original shipping :: You changed your mind. It did not suit the spot you had in mind. It was the wrong size and the dimensions were correctly stated on the product page. You ordered two and only needed one.

Change-of-mind return postage typically runs $8 to $14 for small items and $25 to $40 for the bird bath or the spade, paid directly by you to the carrier. We do not mark it up and we do not take a cut.

If your order originally qualified for free shipping over $49 and a change-of-mind return drops the kept portion below $49, we do not retrospectively charge you the $6.95. We think clawing that back is a sharp practice.

Refunds - method and timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card or send a cheque, because that is how payment-card chargeback protection works.

Order cancelled before shipping
Refunded in full within 7 working days of the cancellation, including any shipping charge.
Returned item, we pay postage
Refunded in full including original shipping, within 5 business days of the parcel arriving with us.
Returned item, change of mind
Price of the goods refunded within 5 business days of arrival. Original shipping is not refunded.
Damaged on arrival, refund instead of replacement
Refunded in full including shipping, within 3 business days of us seeing your photographs. You do not have to wait for the item to travel back.

After we process a refund, your bank or card issuer takes its own time to show it - usually three to five business days, occasionally a full statement cycle. That part is outside our control, but we will send you the refund reference and you are welcome to give it to your bank.

Exchanges

We do not run a formal exchange process, because with a sixteen-product catalogue it is slower than the alternative. Place a new order for the item you want, and return the first one under this policy. That way you are not waiting for one parcel to cross the country before the other sets off.

If the exchange is because we sent the wrong item, we ship the correct one straight away - you are not placing a second order and you are not waiting.

Damaged and defective items

Report transit damage within 14 days of delivery with a photograph of the item and the outer carton. For something that fails in normal use, tell us as soon as it happens and within the 30-day window; we will ask what happened and look at photographs, and if it is a manufacturing fault we replace or refund at our cost.

We do not treat these as covered faults, and we would rather be clear now: cast stone cracked by water left to freeze in it, terracotta cracked by frost, carbon steel rusted from being put away wet, bamboo split after a season in standing rain, and cord or wood degraded by long outdoor exposure. Each of those is named as a care requirement on the relevant product page, and following those instructions is what keeps the item covered.

Cancellations

Before your order ships, call (828) 555-0142 or email hello@porchlightandpine.com with your order number and we will cancel it and refund you in full within 7 working days. We will not ask why.

Once you have a tracking number the parcel has gone and it becomes a return instead. You can refuse delivery if you prefer; the parcel comes back to us and we refund the goods, but refused deliveries take two to three weeks to find their way home, so emailing us is faster.

If we cannot resolve it

Email hello@porchlightandpine.com with "Escalation" in the subject line and your order number. One of the two owners will read it and reply within two business days. We are a small enough business that this is not a department, it is a person.

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under the consumer protection law of your state.
