Where we ship

Porchlight &amp; Pine ships within the United States only. That includes all fifty states, the District of Columbia, and Puerto Rico. Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico are charged at the same flat rate as the lower forty-eight and qualify for free shipping on the same terms, although ground transit to those destinations takes several days longer and we will say so on your confirmation.

We ship to PO boxes and to APO, FPO and DPO military addresses using the United States Postal Service. Two items are an exception: the Stone Pedestal Bird Bath and the Ash-Handle Garden Spade exceed the size limits for military and PO box delivery, and they need a street address. If you order one of those to a PO box we will telephone you rather than cancel the order quietly.

We do not ship outside the United States, we do not ship to freight forwarders, and we cannot ship to a country that is not the United States even if the billing card is American.

What shipping costs

Standard shipping is a flat $6.95 per order regardless of how many items are in it and regardless of which state it is going to. Standard shipping is free on orders over $49 before tax. Expedited shipping is a flat $16.95 per order, and there is no free threshold on expedited service.

There is no handling fee, no packaging fee, no fuel surcharge and no minimum order. The number you see in the cart before checkout is the number you pay, other than sales tax, which is calculated at checkout based on your delivery address where applicable.

Oversize items
The Stone Pedestal Bird Bath (41 lb) and the Ash-Handle Garden Spade (38 in long) ship at the same flat $6.95. We absorb the oversize cost rather than surprising you with it at the last step.
Multiple parcels
If your order needs more than one box, you still pay one shipping charge. You may receive more than one tracking number.

How long it takes

There are two separate clocks and it is worth keeping them apart: how long we take to pack your order, and how long the carrier takes to move it.

Our processing time

- Most items: packed and handed to the carrier within 1 to 2 business days of your order. - Stone Pedestal Bird Bath and Ash-Handle Garden Spade: 2 to 3 business days, because they are packed differently and go by ground. - Orders placed after 2:00pm Eastern Time, at a weekend, or on a federal holiday start their clock on the next business day.

Carrier transit time

These are the carrier's own estimates for time in transit after we hand the parcel over. They are estimates and not guarantees, and they do not include our processing time.

  • Standard ground, lower forty-eight states: 3 to 6 business days
  • Standard ground, Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico: 6 to 12 business days
  • Expedited: 1 to 3 business days (lower forty-eight only)
  • USPS to PO boxes and APO/FPO/DPO: 4 to 10 business days, and military addresses can take considerably longer once the parcel enters the military mail system, which is outside any carrier's tracking

Adding the two clocks together, a standard order to a street address in the lower forty-eight usually arrives four to eight business days after you place it.

Carriers and tracking

We ship by USPS Ground Advantage, USPS Priority Mail, UPS Ground and UPS 2nd Day Air. We choose between them on size, weight and destination, and we do not offer a choice of carrier at checkout because for most of our parcels it would not change either the price or the arrival date.

You will get two emails: an order confirmation when you place the order, and a shipping confirmation with the carrier's name and a tracking number when the parcel leaves us. Tracking usually begins showing movement within 24 hours of that email; a tracking number that has not updated overnight is normal and not a sign of a problem.

If you have not received a shipping confirmation within four business days of ordering, something has gone wrong on our end. Please call (828) 555-0142 or email hello@porchlightandpine.com and we will find out what.

If your order is delayed

This section exists because the Federal Trade Commission's Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule sets out exactly what a seller has to do when it cannot ship on time, and we would rather write our obligations down than leave them implied.

  • We only advertise shipping times we have a reasonable basis to meet. Where we state no time at all, we will ship within 30 days of receiving your properly completed order.
  • If we cannot ship by the date we promised, we will contact you on or before that date with either a definite revised shipping date or a statement that we cannot give one, and with a plain explanation that you may cancel for a full refund at no cost to you.
  • If the revised date is 30 days or less beyond the original date, you do not have to reply. Silence is treated as consent to the short delay, and you keep the right to cancel at any time before the item ships.
  • If the revised date is more than 30 days beyond the original, or if we cannot give a revised date at all, your order is automatically cancelled and refunded unless you tell us expressly that you want to wait.
  • If we then cannot meet the revised date either, we will write to you again before it passes. At that second stage silence is not consent: unless you expressly agree to wait further, we cancel and refund.
  • Cancelling is deliberately easy. Reply to the delay email, call (828) 555-0142, or email hello@porchlightandpine.com. We will not ask you for a reason.

When we cancel an order for delay, you are refunded to the original payment method within 7 working days of the cancellation, and we do not deduct anything.

Lost, late and damaged parcels

### Damaged in transit Email hello@porchlightandpine.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photograph of both the damaged item and the outer carton. We will send a replacement if we have the item in stock, or refund you in full if we do not. We pay all postage either way, and you do not need to return the broken item unless we specifically ask you to. Please keep the packaging until we have replied, because the carrier sometimes wants to see it.

Tracking says delivered but nothing arrived

Please check with anyone else at the address and look in the usual alternative spots first, because most of these turn up within a day. If it has not appeared after three business days, contact us. We will open a claim with the carrier and, whatever the carrier decides, we will either replace the item or refund you. We do not make the customer chase the carrier.

Parcel stopped moving in transit

If tracking has not updated for seven consecutive business days, tell us and we will open a lost-in- transit claim. Replacement or refund is your choice.

Wrong item shipped

That is our mistake. We send a prepaid return label and the correct item, and you are not out of pocket for anything.

Address accuracy

Please check your delivery address before you order. If a parcel is returned to us as undeliverable because the address was wrong or incomplete, we will refund the goods in full and deduct the actual return-transit cost the carrier charged us, which we will show you on the carrier's invoice. If the mistake is ours, we reship at our own cost.

Once an order has shipped we cannot change the address. If you spot a mistake before it ships, call us straight away and we will fix it.

Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your delivery address, where applicable. It is shown as a separate line before you confirm payment, never rolled into the item price or the shipping charge. As a North Carolina business we collect North Carolina sales and use tax, and we collect tax in other states where economic-nexus thresholds require it.
