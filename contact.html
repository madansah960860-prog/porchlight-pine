Standard shipping is a flat $6.95 anywhere in the United States, and it is free on orders over $49. There is no handling fee and no surcharge added at the last step. Sales tax is calculated at checkout where applicable. Full detail is on our shipping policy page.

We pack most orders within one to two business days, and the cast stone bird bath and the border spade within two to three business days. After that, standard ground service usually takes three to six business days to arrive, and expedited service one to three. Those delivery windows are the carrier's estimate, not a guarantee. If we cannot ship within the time we promised, we will email you before the promised date with a revised date and your right to cancel for a full refund.

Yes to all four. Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico are charged at the same flat $6.95 and qualify for free shipping over $49 in exactly the same way, although ground transit takes longer. We ship to PO boxes and to APO and FPO addresses by USPS. We do not ship outside the United States.

You have 30 days from the day your order is delivered to start a return, on anything unused and in its original packaging. Email hello@porchlightandpine.com with your order number and we will send you the address and a returns slip. The full terms, including who pays the return postage, are on the returns and refunds page.

If the item arrived damaged, faulty, or is not what you ordered, we pay - we send you a prepaid label and you are refunded in full including the original shipping. If you simply changed your mind, you pay the return postage and we refund the price of the goods. We never charge a restocking fee.
