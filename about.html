About Porchlight & Pine

A two-person shop in Asheville, North Carolina, selling a short list of things for the yard and the front step.

Who we are

Porchlight &amp; Pine is a two-person shop in west Asheville, North Carolina. We started at a Saturday market on Haywood Road with a folding table and a dozen cedar feeders, and we still pack every order ourselves in the same building we store the stock in.

We are a retailer. Some of what we sell we build — the cedar feeders and nest boxes are cut and assembled here — and the rest we buy in from small suppliers and importers. We tell you which is which on the product page, and we do not put a country-of-origin claim on anything we cannot document.

How we choose what to stock

We are deliberately a short list. Sixteen products, four categories. A thing gets onto the list only if it passes three tests.

  1. We can state what it is made of. Not "premium materials" - the actual species of wood, the actual grade of steel, the actual coating.
  2. It can be cleaned. Anything that holds seed, nectar or water needs washing, and a feeder you cannot take apart is a feeder that stops getting washed. If it does not come apart, it does not go on the list.
  3. The failure mode is honest. Terracotta is not frost-proof and cast stone cracks if you let water freeze in it. We would rather write that on the product page than take the order and deal with it in March.

What we will not do is carry something because it is cheap to buy and easy to describe vaguely. There is a whole category of garden goods that exists only to be photographed, and it is not on this site.

Where orders ship from

Every order leaves our building at 2147 Hollowbrook Lane in Asheville, North Carolina, packed by one of us. We ship within the United States only, including Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico, and we ship to PO boxes and APO/FPO addresses. Full detail is on the shipping policy page.

Most orders go out within one to two business days. The two heavy items - the cast stone bird bath and the border spade - take two to three business days because they are packed differently and go by ground.

What we do not claim

We think it is worth being explicit about this, because a lot of garden retail is not.

  • No health claims. Nothing we sell relieves, prevents, treats or improves any condition. Our hand tools have handle dimensions, not therapeutic properties.
  • No wildlife guarantees. We will tell you a feeder has four ports and a 1-inch perch lip. We will not tell you which birds will come, because that depends on your trees and your neighbourhood, not on our feeder.
  • No reviews or ratings. You will not find stars, testimonials or "customer favourite" badges anywhere on this site. We have not been trading long enough to have a body of honest reviews, so we show none at all rather than invent them.
  • No origin claims. We do not print "Made in USA" on anything. Where an item is made here we can tell you so on the telephone with the supplier's paperwork in front of us; we will not print it as marketing.
  • No fake urgency. No countdown timers, no "only 2 left" counters, no crossed-out prices we never actually charged.

Accessibility commitment

We built this site to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.1 at Level AA, and we test it that way rather than assuming.

In practice that means body text starts at 18 pixels with a line height of 1.7 and no light-grey small print anywhere; text contrast is at least 4.5 to 1 and usually better than 7 to 1; every button says what it does in words rather than relying on an icon; every interactive target is at least 48 pixels tall; the whole site works from the keyboard with a visible focus outline; there is a skip-to-content link at the top of every page; every image carries meaningful alternative text; and the layout reflows down to a 320-pixel-wide screen without horizontal scrolling and stays usable at 200 per cent zoom.

There are no pop-ups on page load, no automatically-playing carousels, no infinite scroll and no motion that you cannot stop.

If any part of this site is hard for you to use, please tell us - call (828) 555-0142 or email hello@porchlightandpine.com - and we will fix it and help you place the order in the meantime.
