What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal information Porchlight & Pine Goods LLC collects when you use porchlightandpine.com or telephone us, why we collect it, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can require us to do about it. It covers the website, our order emails, our seasonal mailing list and our telephone orders.

We are a small retailer, not an advertising business. We do not run behavioural advertising, we do not host third-party advertising pixels, and we do not sell or share personal information. Those are statements of fact about how the site is built, not aspirations.

Information we collect

### Information you give us Order information :: Your name, delivery address, billing address, email address and telephone number. We need these to pack a parcel, address it, and tell you when it left. Payment information :: Card details are entered on our payment processor's own hosted page and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers. We receive only the last four digits, the card brand, the authorisation result and the amount, which is what we need to match a payment to an order and to issue a refund. Contact form messages :: Your name, email address, the order number you choose to give, and what you wrote. Mailing list :: Your email address, and the date you asked to be added. Telephone orders :: The same order information as above, taken down by hand.

Information collected automatically

This site is static HTML with one small JavaScript file. It sets no cookies of its own and includes no third-party analytics, advertising or social media scripts. There is no Google Analytics tag, no advertising pixel and no embedded social widget on any page.

Our web host records standard server logs for security and diagnostics: the requesting IP address, the date and time, the file requested, the HTTP status code, the referring page and the browser's user-agent string. Those logs are kept for 30 days and then deleted.

Your cart is stored in your own browser using localStorage, which is a browser storage feature and not a cookie. It stays on your device, it is never transmitted to us, and clearing your browser data removes it. We cannot see what is in your cart.

How we use your information

- To take, pack, address and ship your order, and to handle returns and refunds. - To send you transactional email: an order confirmation, a shipping confirmation with tracking, a delay notice if one becomes necessary, and refund confirmations. - To answer the question you sent us, or to call you back. - To send you the seasonal mailing list, if and only if you asked for it. - To keep the records that tax and accounting law require us to keep. - To detect and prevent fraud and to keep the site secure.

We do not use your information to build an advertising profile, we do not do automated decision-making, and we do not run any kind of profiling.

Cookies and similar technologies

We set no first-party cookies and permit no third-party cookies. There is nothing to consent to and no cookie banner, because there is no tracking.

Two non-cookie technologies are in use and both stay on your own device: localStorage holds your cart contents, and your browser's ordinary HTTP cache holds our images and stylesheet so pages load faster on a second visit.

If you use a Global Privacy Control signal or a Do Not Track header, we honour it by default, because there is no cross-context tracking here for it to switch off.

Who we share information with

We share the minimum necessary with a small number of service providers, each of which is contractually a service provider or processor - they may use the information only to perform the service and may not use it for their own purposes.

Payment processor
Receives your name, billing address, email and card details directly from you on its own hosted page. Needed to take payment and issue refunds.
Shipping carriers (USPS, UPS)
Receive your name, delivery address, telephone number and parcel weight. Needed to deliver the parcel and provide tracking.
Email delivery provider
Receives your email address and the content of transactional emails and the mailing list.
Web host
Holds the static site files and the server logs described above.
Accountant and tax authorities
Receive order and payment records as required by law.

We will also disclose information where we are legally required to - a valid subpoena, court order or lawful request - and in that case we will tell you unless we are legally prohibited from doing so. If the business were ever sold, personal information could transfer to the buyer as part of it, and we would notify you by email before that happened.

We have never sold personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we have no plans to. We do not sell or share the personal information of anyone, including anyone under 16.

Your rights under US state privacy laws

Depending on where you live, state law may give you some or all of the following rights. We honour these rights for every customer, in every state, rather than only where a statute compels us.

  • Right to know. Ask us what categories and specific pieces of personal information we hold about you, where we got them, why we have them, and who we have disclosed them to.
  • Right to delete. Ask us to delete what we hold, subject to the records tax, accounting and fraud-prevention law requires us to keep.
  • Right to correct. Ask us to fix information that is inaccurate.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. There is no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link on this site because the practice it would switch off does not occur.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as state law defines it - no Social Security numbers, no financial account numbers, no precise geolocation, no biometric, genetic or health data.
  • Right to non-discrimination. Exercising any of these rights will never get you a worse price, a slower service or a refused order.
  • Right to appeal. If we refuse a request, we will tell you why and how to appeal, and an owner will review the appeal.

These rights are available to residents of California under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, and on substantially similar terms to residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and other states with comprehensive privacy laws.

How to make a request

Email hello@porchlightandpine.com with "Privacy request" in the subject line, or call (828) 555-0142 during Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm Eastern Time. Tell us which right you are exercising and give us the email address or order number you used, so we can find your records.

We will acknowledge within 10 days and respond substantively within 45 days. If we genuinely need longer we will tell you inside those 45 days and take up to a further 45, for a maximum of 90 days in total. If you are asking on someone else's behalf as an authorised agent, we will ask for written proof of your authority and for verification from the person themselves.

We do not charge for a privacy request. If a request were manifestly unfounded or excessive we could charge a reasonable fee, but we would explain and agree it with you first.

How long we keep information

Order records :: 7 years, because tax and accounting rules require it. Payment authorisation records (last four digits only) :: 7 years, with the order. Contact form and email correspondence :: 24 months from the last message, then deleted. Mailing list address :: Until you unsubscribe, then removed within 10 days. Server logs :: 30 days. Return and refund records :: 7 years, with the order.

Security

Our website is served over HTTPS, so traffic between your browser and the site is encrypted. Payment details are entered on the processor's own PCI-DSS compliant hosted page and never reach our systems. Access to order records is limited to the two owners, each with an individual account and multi-factor authentication. Paper records, where they exist, are locked.

No system is perfectly secure and we will not pretend otherwise. If a breach affected your personal information we would notify you and the relevant state authorities as state breach-notification law requires, without undue delay.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience store for garden and porch goods. It is not directed to children under 13, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and nothing on the site is designed to appeal particularly to children.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has given us information, email hello@porchlightandpine.com and we will remove it and confirm to you that we have.

Placing an order requires you to be at least 18, as set out in our terms of service.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date at the top and, for any change that materially affects your rights, email everyone on our mailing list and place a notice on the home page for 30 days. We will not apply a materially worse policy retroactively to information already collected.
